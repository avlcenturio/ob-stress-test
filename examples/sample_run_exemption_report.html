2. Summary

In accordance with Article 32(2) of the RTS on SCA & CSC and EBA Guideline 4, Example Bank A/S stress tested its dedicated interface on 19 May 2026 to assess the impact of an extremely high number of requests on the availability and performance of the interface and on its defined service level targets.

The dedicated interface met the stress testing requirements

16,336 requests over 5 min 12 s with 100.00% success, an average response time of 115 ms and 95% of responses within 205 ms. The test sustained 100 concurrent users, 5.0× the current peak of 20 concurrent users on the PSU interface.

3. Stress testing approach

The stress test was performed by Example Bank performance engineering team, with the Open Banking platform team following an established procedure. In line with the EBA guide on the exemption conditions, it covered:

  1. access by multiple PISPs, CBPIIs and AISPs;
  2. an extremely high number of requests from TPPs in a short period without failing;
  3. an extremely high number of concurrent sessions for payment initiation, confirmation of funds and account information;
  4. requests for large volumes of data.

Load basis

The basis for the load model is the highest number of concurrently active customers on the online and mobile banking channels over the last 12 months (20 PSUs, from production analytics). The test applied five times that load to demonstrate headroom for a growing TPP market.

11. Conclusion

The stress testing results are accepted as successful. The dedicated interface of Example Bank A/S handled every requirement in EBA Guideline 4.2(a) to (d) within its service level targets, and its performance under stress was at least equal to that of the PSU interface.
